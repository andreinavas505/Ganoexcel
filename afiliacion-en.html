Before you register

How to start your enrollment

Registration options vary by country. Use the official portal for your market and confirm current details during the process.

STEP 01

Review your options

Review the available enrollment packages or starter kits. Check what each option includes and whether an initial purchase is required.

STEP 02

Review the requirements

Review the contract, policies and required documents. The official Colombia portal indicates that the process includes a multi-level marketing agreement and identity documentation.

STEP 03

Confirm the total cost

Before paying, check the selected products' price, taxes, shipping and total cost. Amounts depend on the option you choose and current terms.

For product buyers

Want to buy Gano Excel products at a discount?

Some enrollment options may provide access to member or wholesale pricing, depending on the package, enrollment type and current policies. Packages differ, so check whether the option includes products, requires an initial purchase or involves ongoing purchases before you register.

OPTION 01

Compare the initial cost

Check the kit or package price, which products are included, and the total cost with taxes and shipping.

OPTION 02

Verify member pricing

Ask which products qualify for member pricing, what requirements apply, and whether minimum or recurring purchases are required.

OPTION 03

Choose based on your goals

If you only want to use the products, compare the total cost with a regular purchase. If you also want to distribute them, review the agreement and business policies before deciding.
